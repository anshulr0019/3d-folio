# Part 6 — character concept review

Status: concept exploration, awaiting the user's selection before model replacement.

Primary visual source: the approved forest/oak/linen miniature studio and project campus. Preserve matte materials, restrained color, clear architectural shapes, and a readable silhouette at the current 2.4-unit character height. The previous yellow-shirt child is not the shape target.

The approved brief calls for a stylized adult developer, balanced proportions, forest/charcoal clothing, sneakers, and one recognizable accessory. These concepts are original fictional characters, not personal likenesses.

| Direction | Outfit and accessory | Design reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Campus workwear (recommended) | Forest overshirt, ivory tee, charcoal trousers, off-white sneakers, compact canvas backpack | The light backpack gives a distinct rear silhouette under the normal elevated camera; colors come from the approved studio/campus. |
| B — Studio hoodie | Charcoal hoodie, muted forest trousers, off-white sneakers, headphones around neck | A relaxed developer silhouette; the hoodie and headphones are more familiar but less distinctive. |
| C — Utility layers | Forest utility vest over an ivory long-sleeve, charcoal trousers, off-white sneakers, charcoal beanie | Strong layered color blocks and a distinctive head outline, with a more utilitarian character. |

All options: adult anatomy around 6–6.5 heads tall, simple face, short dark hair where visible, minimal surface noise, no logos/neon, feet and hands fully visible, production-feasible garments. Medium warm skin echoes the existing fictional avatar without claiming the user's appearance.

Media: three separate raster concept boards, one per direction, with a large front three-quarter view and smaller rear/elevated views of the same character. Built-in image generation is used for concept imagery only. Images do not contain geometry, a skeleton, or working animation.

Downstream model requirements, after selection: actual rigged geometry, compatible or retargeted idle/walk/run/jump clips, readable overhead silhouette, consistent identity across quality settings, and fresh grounding/stride calibration. The current Mixamo-named asset pipeline cannot accept an arbitrary image or unrelated rig unchanged.
