# Character concept generation prompts

Execution: built-in image_gen tool, three separate generation calls, opaque backgrounds. Each call used the shared prompt followed by its corresponding option prompt below. These are approval images, not runtime character assets.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original 3D game-character visual concept board, for a premium interactive developer portfolio; this is artwork for design approval, not a screenshot or a functioning 3D model.
Create a landscape 3:2 concept sheet on a plain warm linen background (#f6f3e8), art-directed like a polished small independent game's character design presentation. One adult fictional male developer in his mid twenties, medium warm brown skin, short dark textured hair where visible, understated friendly adult face, dark eyes, adult shoulders and legs, about 6.5 heads tall. Adult anatomy is essential: no large baby head, no huge eyes, no toddler, no chibi proportions. Simplified sculpted facial planes, broad readable shapes, clean construction, matte fabric and rubber materials, subtly beveled shapes, premium stylized 3D rendering, no shiny vinyl-toy finish, no photorealism.
Composition: a large full-body front three-quarter view in a relaxed neutral A pose on the left; on the right a smaller full-body rear three-quarter view and a genuinely small elevated/isometric view showing the character from a portfolio's normal distant game camera. All three views show exactly the SAME character, identical outfit details and proportions, fully visible hands and feet. Keep limbs separated and hands empty. Soft neutral daylight and restrained contact shadows; no dramatic cinematic lighting, no floating particles. The elevated view must demonstrate readable simplified silhouette, not introduce a different giant-headed toy.
World context is a miniature oak-and-sage studio and architectural garden campus: forest green #355c4b, warm charcoal #30352f, ivory #e9dfc7, oak/brass details only where specified. No scenery or extra props. No logos, no random text, no purple/cyan neon, no gradients, no headset glow, no weapons. Only a small clean heading at top with the option title, no annotation clutter. This is an original fictional avatar, NOT a portrait or a likeness of any real person.

## A — Campus workwear

OPTION TITLE (verbatim): "A / CAMPUS WORKWEAR"
Outfit: a matte forest green overshirt worn open over a plain ivory crew-neck T-shirt, straight charcoal trousers ending neatly at the ankles, clean off-white low-top sneakers with slightly substantial cream rubber soles. Sleeve cuffs simple and unrolled. One signature accessory ONLY: a compact ivory canvas backpack with oak-tan straps, a simple flat pocket and one small dull brass buckle. It must be clearly visible on the rear view and readable from above, with front shoulder straps visible on the main view. Bag is fitted closely to the back for plausible walking/running, not oversized. Natural short textured dark hair with an asymmetric, modest swept forelock. Refined grown-up character, calm stance, not a fashion-ad pose. Reproduce the same two overshirt pockets, seams, shoes, and backpack consistently across views.

## B — Studio hoodie

OPTION TITLE (verbatim): "B / STUDIO HOODIE"
Outfit: a structured matte charcoal hoodie, hood DOWN with the short textured hair and neck visible, small flat kangaroo pocket and restrained simple drawcords; straight muted forest green trousers, clean off-white low-top sneakers with cream rubber soles. One signature accessory ONLY: compact matte dark headphones resting around the neck, with muted sage ear-pad faces, no microphone and no glow. No backpack, no glasses, no hat, no jewelry. Hoodie has a precise silhouette and sensible adult fit, not a huge shapeless garment. Hands empty, hair sculpted in a few clear directional locks. Reproduce identical hood, drawcords, pocket, headphones and shoes consistently in all views.

## C — Utility layers

OPTION TITLE (verbatim): "C / UTILITY LAYERS"
Outfit: a clean matte forest green utility vest worn open over a plain ivory long-sleeve crew-neck shirt; straight charcoal trousers, clean off-white low-top sneakers with cream rubber soles. Two simple broad flat vest pockets, no straps or military styling. One signature accessory ONLY: a close-fitting charcoal knit beanie with a simple folded cuff and no logo; some short dark hair may show at the forehead. No bag, no headphones, no glasses, no jewelry. The vest/ivory sleeve color blocks and beanie must be easy to recognize from the elevated camera. Adult build, purposeful but relaxed posture, refined clean simplified materials. Reproduce identical vest pockets, beanie and shoes consistently across all views.
